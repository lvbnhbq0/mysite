**This week** mostly revolved around something simple and happy: **my birthday on 15 May** — not a huge party, but I still wanted the day to feel intentional instead of improvised.

## Lists, not chaos

I kept a **shared note** with groceries, a **rough timetable** for the day (when to pick up the cake, when to tidy the kitchen, when to actually stop cleaning and get dressed), and a short **gift checklist** so I would not panic-buy things nobody asked for. Crossing items off sounds trivial, but it stopped my brain from rehearsing the same worries on loop.

## Small treats

I baked a trial batch of **cupcakes** mid-week to test frosting consistency, picked a **playlist** that is ninety percent nostalgic nonsense, and reserved a table at a **small café** I like — nothing Michelin, just good light and room to talk. The week felt full, but in a soft way: errands as a love language to my future self on Thursday morning.
